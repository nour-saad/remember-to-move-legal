Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

World Cloud Solutions provides Remember to Move. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, contact us, or visit this page.

Your movement history and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not require an account. Apple and RevenueCat process subscription information. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your movement session times, durations, selected movements, completion or dismissal status, and preferences such as reminder schedules, quiet hours, sounds, appearance, and Live Activity settings. Streaks and statistics are calculated from your session history. For installations with a previously granted local free-access period, a local record retains its original four-day or seven-day deadline and a last-seen timestamp. New subscription trials are handled through Apple. The app also stores information needed to coordinate reminders and Live Activity actions.

We do not upload your movement history or preferences to our own servers or to RevenueCat. Device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple settings. The app does not read or write Apple Health/HealthKit data or request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Subscriptions and purchase information

We use Apple’s App Store and RevenueCat to offer subscriptions, validate purchases, restore access, and determine subscription status. RevenueCat creates a random app user identifier without requiring you to register. It processes purchase and subscription records, such as product identifiers, transaction or receipt information, purchase and expiration dates, renewal status, currency, and associated technical information such as app and operating-system versions and last-seen activity.

This processing supports app access, purchase restoration, fraud prevention, and subscription reporting. Subscription checks may contact RevenueCat when the app opens, including during your free-access period. We do not send your name or email address to RevenueCat through the app. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card information. We do not include advertising or movement-analytics SDKs.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy for information about those services.

Reminders and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. Live Activities show your current session on supported system surfaces, including the Lock Screen. These displays may be visible to someone who can see your device. You can change reminder and Live Activity preferences in the app and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Support communications

If you email younifyr@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Support email is processed by our email provider, Google. Please avoid sending payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

This website

This page uses no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. It is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until removed. Deleting the app rather than offloading it removes its app-container data; backups or system-managed records may remain. Manage backups through your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription or erase Apple or RevenueCat transaction records. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

We retain support correspondence and service records for as long as needed to provide support, maintain subscription access, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal requirements. Apple and service providers may retain records under their own obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we can identify and control. Because the app uses a random identifier, we may need limited purchase-related information to locate a subscription record; we will not ask for your Apple password or payment-card details.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including restricting or objecting to processing, obtaining a copy of your data, or making a complaint to your local privacy authority. We process information to provide the service and respond to requests, for legitimate service-security and support purposes, and to meet legal obligations, as applicable. Providers may process information outside your country.

Security and policy updates

We use the platform’s storage protections and service-provider security measures, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.

Contact & Support

For app help or privacy requests, contact World Cloud Solutions at younifyr@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

Remember to Move uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

New users require an active subscription confirmed through Apple. Eligible new subscribers may receive a seven-day free trial when offered on the purchase screen. After the Apple trial, the subscription renews at the price and billing period shown at purchase unless cancelled. Previously granted local four-day or seven-day free-access periods keep their original expiry dates and do not automatically convert to paid subscriptions. Subscriptions begin only after you confirm with Apple and renew according to the terms shown in Apple’s purchase screen. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
